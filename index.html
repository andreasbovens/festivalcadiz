What is Bachata Week?

After 11 amazing years as Sensual Week, we have a new name that represents how the bachata dance community has evolved.

Bachata Week is a week of focused, high-quality bachata training in small groups with selected dancers, taught by the world’s leading Sensual, Dominican, and Fusion artists.

If you want mastery, clean technique, deeper connection, better musicality, confidence, and a week that fills you with inspiration, friendships and growth… this is for you!

Location

Classes will be held in the beautiful Hotel Parador de Cádiz , and most parties will take place in the same location as well.

Accommodation is available at Hotel Parador as well as in Cádiz’s historical centre, where you’ll find a wide range of hotels, B&Bs and apartments, all within walking distance of the venue.

What you’ll get

  • 10 hours of small-group training with headliners
  • Additional workshops & activities throughout the week
  • Balanced partner groups (leaders/followers)

Price: €440 (Payment can be done in four instalments of €110.)

How to join?

Admission is through a video application. First, we’ll do an initial pass of the video applications and if you’re selected, we’ll send you an invite to purchase a pass and select your preferred Focus Area:

  • Couple Technique: You work directly in partnerwork, developing the mechanics that make your dancing feel comfortable, controlled and musical.
  • Body Technique: You learn to move better individually, and then apply those tools in partnerwork.

Once you’ve purchased your ticket, we’ll do a detailed review of your video and you’ll be placed in a track best suited for your learning. We review each video carefully to keep the level, balance, and learning experience that makes this week special.
