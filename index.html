A unique bachata experience

After 11 amazing years as Sensual Week, we have a new name that represents how our dance and our community have evolved.

Bachata Week is:

  • 🌍 A global gathering of dancers from over all over the world.
  • 🏖️ A week by the beach in beautiful Cádiz, Spain.
  • 💃 Workshops focused on growth: technique, connection and musicality, not just combinations.
  • 🎉 Unforgettable social dancing every night.
  • 🤝 A real community built on humility, learning and connection.

Why "Bachata Week"?

This new name celebrates how Bachata has evolved and the global community we've built together.

Location

Classes will be held in the beautiful Hotel Parador de Cádiz , and most parties will take place in the same location as well.

Accommodation is available at Hotel Parador as well as in Cádiz’s historical centre, where you’ll find a wide range of hotels, B&Bs and apartments, all within walking distance of the venue.
