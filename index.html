A unique bachata experience

Welcome to Cádiz, a magical city by the sea, full of sunshine, music, and good vibes. Imagine golden beaches, warm nights, and dancers from all over the world learning, growing, and celebrating bachata together for one unforgettable week.

At Bachata Week, you’ll improve your technique, style, musicality, and connection with some of the world’s top bachata teachers and dancers. There’s time to train, but also time to dance by the beach, enjoy the sunsets, and create lifelong memories with old and new friends.

Every evening, we’ll have incredible parties that many describe as the best bachata socials in the world. Dancers from over fifty countries come together each year to share their love for bachata, to connect, and to grow together — all in the city where Bachata Sensual was born.

After more than ten years of creating Sensual Week, Yenny & Andreas continue the tradition with a fresh name, Bachata Week, celebrating all bachata styles, from traditional to sensual.

Pack your dance shoes and your swimsuit. Cádiz is waiting for you!

Location

Classes will be held in the beautiful Hotel Parador de Cádiz , and most parties will take place in the same location as well.

Accommodation is available at Hotel Parador as well as in Cádiz’s historical centre, where you’ll find a wide range of hotels, B&Bs and apartments, all within walking distance of the venue.
